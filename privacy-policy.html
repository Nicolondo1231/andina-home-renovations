Privacy Policy

Andina Home Renovations LLC · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Andina Home Renovations LLC ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate through our website, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, by phone or by text, we collect the information you give us: your name, phone number, email, town or address, and details about your project.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, schedule and confirm your estimate, send appointment reminders, provide our services, and answer your questions.

Text messages (SMS)

If you provide your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Andina Home Renovations LLC about your estimate request, appointment confirmations and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our messaging provider), and never sold.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with service providers that help us operate (for example, scheduling and messaging tools), only for those purposes, or when required by law.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to serve you or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time by emailing andinahomerenovations@gmail.com or calling (201) 981-7239. To stop text messages, reply STOP.
